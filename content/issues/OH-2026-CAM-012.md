## Summary

`IsUint8Regex` is a bool gate for decimals in `[0, 255]`. After
`isIntegerRegex` it calls `std::stoi`, which throws `std::out_of_range` on
values that do not fit in `int` (e.g. `"2147483648"`). Callers have no
try/catch, so malformed camera XML `type` aborts parse. Same class as
`IsDoubleRegex` (`DTS2026071303295`) and `UpdateBasicInfoForStream`
(`DTS2026071411883`). Confirmed and fixed under `DTS2026082239652`.

## Vulnerable Code

`camera_util.cpp` `IsUint8Regex` (~572): digit-run accept, then uncaught
`std::stoi`.

## Impact

Medium — uncaught `stoi` aborts camera XML parse instead of skipping the
node.
