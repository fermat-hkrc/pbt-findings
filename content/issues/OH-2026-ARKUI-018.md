## Summary

On the `spaceUpdated_` path, isolated `UpdatePosMap` sets `adjustOffset_.start`
to the full body delta when `endIndex_ >= 0`. PBT treated item 0’s unchanged
`startPos` as proof that `start` must be 0 (else top “jumps”).

**NON-ISSUE** under `DTS2026091437627`. Maintainers: that is scroll
**compensation** for visible content in the real measure path, not a top jump.
The isolated `start==0` oracle does not hold.

## Developer feedback

> 报告将 adjustOffset.start 理解为首项位置变化，通过孤立调用得出“修改间距会导致顶部跳动”的结论，未考虑真实布局中的可见项测量和滚动补偿流程。该补偿用于保持可见内容位置，属于正常逻辑，报告的测试预期不成立。

## Contract (product)

`adjustOffset.start` participates in the live layout frame (visible-item measure
+ scroll compensation). Non-zero `start` after a gap-only change keeps visible
content put. Do not equate bare helper output with “first item moved.”

## Observation (still real on isolated call)

```cpp
adjustOffset_.start = endIndex_ < 0 ? 0.0f : totalMainSize_ - prevTotalMainSize_;
```

8×200, gap 0→20, covering: isolated call yields `start=140, end=0`.

## How PBT Detected This

`SpaceUpdatedUnmovedTopHasZeroStartAdjust` FAIL on the bare helper.

## Disposition

Align PBT to product (compensation). Do not re-file without a live-frame
witness that visible content actually jumps after measure + `ShiftLayoutWindow`.
